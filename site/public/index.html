How it works

Your instance. Your provider.

Run the MiniCloud dashboard and API on a dedicated VPS. You and your agents use the CLI to manage projects, and MiniCloud provisions machines in your Hetzner, DigitalOcean, or Vultr account. The deployment worker rolls out Docker services, and the workspace gateway connects your browser to the coding VM.

  • PostgreSQL
  • OpenBao
  • OpenTelemetry
